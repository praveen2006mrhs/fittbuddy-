FitBuddy Architecture & Capabilities

Designed as a clean, single-app FastAPI solution with zero bloated frameworks.

📋

7-Day Workout Generation

Generates structured daily routines with sets, reps, warm-ups, and cool-downs powered by the official google-genai SDK.

🔄

Feedback & Revision Loop

Allows users to review, critique, or request adjustments (e.g. sore shoulder, shorter sessions) to generate revised iterations.

📊

Profile & Workout History

Persists user profiles and generated plan iterations in a lightweight, self-contained local SQLite database with SQLAlchemy.

🥗

Nutrition & Recovery Tips

Instant tailored recovery strategies, protein targets, and hydration advice keyed directly to your workout intensity.

🛡️

Admin Management

Authenticated oversight panel for administrators to inspect registered users, plans, and generation metrics.

🔒

Secure Local-First Stack

Strict environment-based secret configuration, no client-side API keys, running strictly on 127.0.0.1.
